# Digital Signal Processing — Assignment 1

Solutions to the first assignment of the Digital Signal Processing (DSP) course, covering discrete-time systems, Fourier analysis, and frequency-domain signal processing.

## Assignment Overview

This assignment explores fundamental concepts in digital signal processing through analytical problem-solving and computational implementation.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Causal LTI Systems

- Analysis of causal linear time-invariant (LTI) systems
- Impulse response and system output
- Z-transform and system function
- Analysis of system properties

### 2. BIBO Stability

- Bounded-Input Bounded-Output (BIBO) stability
- Stability analysis using the impulse response
- Investigation of system behavior

### 3. Discrete-Time Fourier Series (DTFS)

- Calculation of DTFS coefficients
- Representation of periodic discrete-time signals
- Analysis of frequency-domain coefficients

### 4. Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT)

- Computation and comparison of 8-point and 16-point DFTs
- Analysis of DFT magnitude spectra
- Investigation of zero-padding and its effect on spectral representation

### 5. Frequency Resolution and Spectral Leakage

- Investigation of frequency resolution in the DFT
- Analysis of the effects of sampling frequency and sequence length
- Exploration of spectral leakage

- MATLAB

- SymPy
- Digital Signal Processing
- Z-transform
